In this folder are the results of agent testing of 100 levels generated by TOAD-GAN made from the original level-1. 
The generator was premade and comes from the TOAD-GUI project (and also the levels were generated using TOAD-GUI).

mffAstar only failed on generated level 57, which is truly broken and cannot be finished.
Which means mffAstar completed all levels which could be finished in this test and also TOAD-GAN generated only 1
unplayable level from the source original level-1.

On the other hand Baumgarten "LOST" on level 13 and "TIMED_OUT" on levels 34, 57 and 68.
Which means Baumgarten failed on 3 levels which could be completed.
I found out that the agent "mff.agents.astar" "LOST" on level 13 too. 
I also found out that Baumgarten gets stuck only sometimes on the levels 34 and 68.
