In this folder are the results of agent testing of 1000 levels generated by TOAD-GAN made from randomly chosen set of the original levels. 
The generator was premade and comes from the TOAD-GUI project (and also the levels were generated using TOAD-GUI).

######
Main results:

Baumgarten failed 58,2 % levels.
mffAstar-Grid failed 23,9% levels.

This means 76.1% of levels generated by TOAD-GAN were finishable.

(The paper stated:
"We tested the validity of our generated content using the A* agent by Baumgarten who was able to win 65% of randomly sampled levels."
which doesn't really match our results ((maybe it's still within tolerance?)). Still mffAstar-Grid performed better than 65% of completability.)
